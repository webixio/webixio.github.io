Webixio Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Webixio ("we", "our", or "us") provides a personal web hub, bookmark organizer, and focused web launcher utility for mobile devices. We believe privacy is a fundamental right. This policy outlines how Webixio operates with a strict zero-data-collection architecture.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Webixio does NOT collect, harvest, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, unique device identifiers (IDFA/GAID), IP addresses, or browsing history. We do not maintain user accounts, databases, or user profiles.

2. Strictly Local On-Device Storage

All bookmarks, custom portal shortcuts, and history logs created in the app remain strictly on your physical device in sandboxed storage (iOS UserDefaults & AsyncStorage). None of this data is ever synced or transmitted to our servers or third-party cloud infrastructure.

3. Native WebKit Browsing

When you launch a web portal, Webixio utilizes Apple's native WebKit engine (WKWebView). Traffic travels directly between your device and the host of the requested website under standard HTTPS security protocols. Webixio does not operate a proxy service or intercept user browsing sessions.

4. No Advertising or Tracking Frameworks

Webixio does not integrate advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob, AppLovin, or Unity), attribution trackers (AppsFlyer, Branch), or third-party analytics (Google Analytics, Firebase). We never request Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission because we do not track you.

5. Data Deletion & Retention

You can instantly delete all saved bookmarks and browsing history at any time directly in the app via Settings > Clear All Data, or by uninstalling the app from your device.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at support@webixio.app.
